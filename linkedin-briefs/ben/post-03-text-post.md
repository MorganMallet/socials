Charlie Weintraub is a private chef in Austin, Texas. He trained at the Culinary Institute of America and cooked in restaurants before he went private.

"I'd say like almost 50% of the time is cleaning," he told me. He cleans as he goes, so the kitchen is done by the time dinner is served.

He hasn't worn a white chef's coat since. "I wear a t-shirt and jeans every day and an apron."

He sums up what he gives a household in one line: "the luxury feeling is that they don't have to worry about dinner."

If you're writing a brief for a private chef, put the cleaning in it from the start.
