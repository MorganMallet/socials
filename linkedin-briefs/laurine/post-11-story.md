As a nanny I worked very long hours, and some days I was completely exhausted.

I never made a mistake, and I know I could have fallen on the stairs with the little one. When you're that tired, you don't always know it.

Some families think a run of ten or twelve hour days is fine as long as the days off come later. Your mind needs rest along the way too.

Resting is part of doing this job well. You stay focused, and the hours you give the family are better hours.

If you're working long days over the holidays, take the break when it's offered. I know, easier said than done this week!
