When you start a new role, ask your employer for a short meeting on the first morning, before you start anything. What do they expect from you? Are there rooms to leave until they're out of the house? Are some tasks for the morning and others for the afternoon?

Families are often busy and in a rush. Some will show you the kitchen and the bathroom, give you their number and go. So message them the day before and ask if they have fifteen minutes when you arrive, or come in fifteen minutes early.

I talked through the whole first week with Ben on the podcast, if you have a new job starting in January: https://www.householdstaff.agency/podcast/first-week-luxury-home-candidate-corner/
