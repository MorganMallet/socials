CAPTION
If you've applied for a job with us and heard nothing more, I'd like you to see these numbers.

A personal assistant job draws around 105 applications. A chauffeur job, around 103. Even a governess job draws around 22, and only a few candidates go forward to the family.

Being turned down for one job says very little about your background. Most of the candidates I've met at Morgan & Mallet were great. We talk to the family about their needs, their fears, their family situation and the kind of person they want at home. Sometimes a great resume is the wrong match for that particular house.

Live roles are here: https://www.householdstaff.agency/jobs/
