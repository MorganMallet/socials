Can we tell when a resume was written with ChatGPT? I was asked that on a podcast recording, and yes, very often we can.

Honestly, it doesn't change much. The first thing we look for is your experience, at least three years in this kind of work. If it's there, we invite you for an interview.

The family also sees a different resume from the one you sent. We rewrite every candidate's resume in our own Morgan & Mallet format, so each family gets the same clear layout, built from your real background and your personality.

So if AI helps you put your experience into words, use it. Just check that every date and every duty on the page is true, because we check references with your last employers.
