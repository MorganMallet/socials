Planning a move to a new city for work in the new year? Look first at whether the jobs you want are live-in or live-out.

For a live-out job, families want someone who already lives nearby. If you're moving from Nice to Paris and you're certain you'll be there before the start date, we can include you. The search runs on the family's timetable, so your move needs to be settled.

Live-in jobs are more open. You could be in the Alps or Normandy today.

Years ago I placed a candidate in Paris who lived about five hours away. I really wanted it to work for him. What he hadn't told me was that he had no apartment. He stayed with family, there were a lot of problems, and after two months he said a hotel was too expensive and he had to leave the job.

If your move is already organized, great. If not, the family will usually go with someone nearby.
