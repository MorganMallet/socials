Title: 5 resume mistakes I still see
1. Writing one line per job, like "Butler, 2000 to 2015", with no duties underneath.
2. Leaving out the city where you live.
3. Naming your previous employer, which is best left off.
4. Sending a photo from the beach or the bathroom when a plain white wall and work clothes do the job.
5. Sending it with spelling mistakes, so read it once more before you press send.
Footer: Laurine Mallet, co-founder, Morgan & Mallet

CAPTION
Some candidates are much better in an interview than on paper. Their resume says "Butler, 2000 to 2015" and nothing else, and then in the interview they explain everything they handled in that house, in detail.

When we have doubts we call, because we don't want to miss a great candidate. It helps a lot if the recruiter can see it on the page first.

I did the recruiting myself for eight or nine years, and these five came up often.
