Title: Five steps to get started in household work
1. Build three years of experience first. Private homes, luxury hotels and high-end hospitality all count.
2. Make one folder: references, ID, diplomas, certificates, criminal record, and a smiling professional photo.
3. Write one resume that tells your whole story.
4. Explain every gap, in one line.
5. Register with more than one agency if you like, because that's completely fine.
Footer: Laurine Mallet, co-founder, Morgan & Mallet

CAPTION
We ask every candidate for at least three years of experience. It's written clearly on our website, and still some applications come in from candidates who have never worked in service at all.

If that's you, experience in a luxury hotel or a top restaurant can count. Families often value it, because that level of service and etiquette is hard to teach.

The rest is preparation. Have your folder ready before the right job appears, so you can apply the same day.

My own way into this work was as a nanny in London, so I know how daunting that first application feels!

You can register here: https://www.householdstaff.agency/candidates/
