CAPTION
I know candidates don't come into this work only for the money. Still, salaries in this industry are high, and that's a real motivation.

So here are the ranges from our 2025/26 salary report, US and UK, gross per year.

One thing from my side. Ask for what's fair for your skills, your experience and the market. Be reasonable, and respect your employer, however wealthy the family is.
