Title: 7 things I'd tell any nanny starting out
1. Take notes, because you can't remember everything in week one.
2. Ask how this family likes things done, even if you know another way.
3. Expect your first week to run over your hours.
4. Follow the parents' rules for their children, and keep your views on parenting to yourself.
5. Rest when you can, because tiredness is when accidents happen.
6. Check the accommodation before you say yes, because sharing a room with the baby gets very hard.
7. An envelope at the end of the year is a gift. Your salary belongs in a contract.
Footer: Laurine Mallet, co-founder, Morgan & Mallet

CAPTION
Before Morgan and I started the agency, I was a nanny. I worked mainly in London and traveled with families to Greece, France and Spain.

Number 6 is from my own experience. I once had a big room as a nanny and shared it with the baby I looked after. It was very hard. I was younger, so I managed. Today I don't think I'd do it again.
