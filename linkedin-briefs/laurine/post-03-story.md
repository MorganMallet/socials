CAPTION
If you've had a few different professions, you might keep a separate resume for each of them, say one for driving and one for butler work. I understand, and I see it quite often.

Here is what you can do instead.

Put everything on one resume. A candidate once asked me which of his two he should send us, the driver one or the butler one. I told him he has one life, so he has one resume.

When a job is left off, it shows up as a gap, and a gap makes a recruiter stop and wonder. If there was a break in between, for a new baby, a sabbatical or looking after a parent, add one line that says so.

Having done a few different things also shows you're versatile, and families very often ask for exactly that.
