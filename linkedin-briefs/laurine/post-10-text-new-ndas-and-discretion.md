CAPTION
An NDA, or non-disclosure agreement, is a written promise to keep what you see and hear in a family's home to yourself.

When our team went through 1,756 of our own job listings, 14 mentioned one, under 1%. Some roles ask for them far more often. In Los Angeles, 77% of the personal assistant jobs we have placed required one.

Whether you sign one or not, discretion is part of the job. When I talk to candidates about it, many think first of the family's phone calls. It also means you don't talk about your work to your own family and friends, you keep your opinions to yourself, and you can sense when to leave the room.

It's worth it, too. Our salary report found that confidentiality can add 15 to 20% to salaries. Some households even ask staff to leave their phones at the door.
