CAPTION
A lot of families travel over the holidays, and their staff travel with them.

If your team has just flown in with you, give them two days off, even if you've only just landed in New York. It was a long trip for them too.

Treat people with respect, keep the communication good and say thank you. People treated that way tend to stay for the long term.

Staff who travel with you give a lot of their life to the job, so pay them a good salary.

For next year, plan the rota three to six months ahead with your family office or PA. There will always be last-minute trips, and a plan makes them easier to cover.
