CAPTION
Before anything else on a resume, I look at how long the person stayed.

Someone who moves every single year is a red flag for me. I want to see three, four or five years in the same property, and references that back it up.

Then use the trial period properly. See how they handle a stressful moment and a quiet one. If you have a team, find a way for them to spend some time together, maybe an event on a day off. A household runs a lot like a company.
