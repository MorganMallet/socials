CAPTION
In a large, valuable home the housekeeper is one of the most important people on the team. This is how a good one uses the first hour.

She goes to the bedroom while the family is at breakfast, or he does, because it can be a man, and opens the windows and curtains and makes the bed. The rest of the house waits until the family has gone out.

The skill is being there without being in the way.

We look for housekeepers who take initiative with the cleaning, the winter clean and the summer clean included.

It helps to have a real SOP for the role. Usually the house manager writes it, sometimes the family office or the PA.
