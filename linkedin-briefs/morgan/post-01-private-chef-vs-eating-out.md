CAPTION
A family of four in the US who eat breakfast, lunch and dinner at home, five days a week, can have a full-time private chef for about $53 a plate. That covers the chef's salary, the payroll tax and the groceries. The average restaurant booking in the US is $60 a head.

The sums only work when the chef cooks for the whole family, three meals a day. For a couple, or a family of four who eat only lunch and dinner at home, eating out costs less.

And the price leaves a lot out. Eating out with children means traffic, waiting for a table, and other diners close enough to hear your conversation. A chef serves the food your family chooses, at your own table.

Full breakdown: https://www.householdstaff.agency/private-chef-per-plate/
