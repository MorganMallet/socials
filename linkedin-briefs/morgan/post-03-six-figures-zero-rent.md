CAPTION
Half of the live-in jobs we advertised in the US offered $100,000 a year or more, and the staff paid no rent.

The typical live-in salary was $97,500. Live-out was $102,500. Pretty much the same pay, with a home on top.

Where the family lives makes a big difference. In Texas, 35% of our adverts were live-in. In New York City it was 3%. A house in Dallas often has its own staff quarters, and a Manhattan apartment rarely has a spare suite.

Laurine sees families take the value of the room off the salary. A good salary still has to match the market, because good candidates compare offers.

Full figures: https://www.householdstaff.agency/six-figures-zero-rent/
