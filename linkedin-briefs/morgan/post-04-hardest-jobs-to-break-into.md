The hardest household jobs to break into
Out of every 100 applications for each job, how many our recruiters accepted into our candidate network:
Property guard: 10 in 100
Handyman: 13 in 100
Personal assistant: 16 in 100
Chauffeur: 17 in 100
The easiest:
Nanny: 34 in 100
Butler: 35 in 100
Based on 125,994 applications our recruiters screened, January 2022 to October 2026. Source: Morgan & Mallet recruitment data

CAPTION
Personal assistant jobs get more applications than any other household role we advertise. Our recruiters have screened 11,013 of them since 2022, and only 16 in every 100 made it into our candidate network.

Sometimes people don't read the job description properly. They read the salary, decide it's a fancy job and apply, and many of them don't have the private household experience families want.

Butler and nanny applications do much better, at 35 and 34 in every 100. Fewer people apply for those on a whim.

Every candidate we put forward to a family has passed a recruiter's screening, a 90-minute interview and reference checks.
